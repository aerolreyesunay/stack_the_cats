## 2. App content (Policy → App content)

**Ads:** Yes, the app contains ads.

**App access:** "All or some functionality is restricted."
Instructions for reviewers:

```
On first launch the game asks for a birth year. Choose 2000 for the full game, or 2018 to see kids' mode (under 13: anonymous account, made-up name, no sign-in, friends or live matches, child-directed ads). To switch, clear the app's data and open it again.

Full game: it can be played without an account (guest mode, up to 50 cats per game). The Store, leaderboards, friends, live matches and saving progress need a sign-in: tap the Google button on the main menu and use any Google account. No special credentials are needed.
```

**Target audience and content:** ages 6–8, 9–12, 13–15, 16–17 and 18+ (a mixed audience, including children). This puts the app under Google Play's Families policy. How the app meets it:

- A neutral age screen on first launch (birth year, nothing picked in advance). Under 13 gets kids' mode.
- Ads: Google AdMob, which is certified for Families, requested as child-directed for kids (no personalised ads, rated G).
- Kids' mode collects no personal information: an anonymous account with a made-up name, no Google/Facebook sign-in, no photos, no friends, invites, live matches, wagers or chat.
- Privacy policy covers children and how parents can delete their child's data.
- Play Console will ask you to confirm the ads SDK and its settings; answer that kids get child-directed ads from AdMob.

**Content rating questionnaire** (IARC), category: Game

- Violence, fear, sexual content, language, drugs: No
- Gambling: No. (Matches can stake in-game treats, but outcomes depend on skill, and treats can't be bought or cashed out.)
- Users can interact or exchange information: Yes (names and photos on leaderboards, friends, live matches; no chat)
- Shares the user's current location with other users: No
- Allows purchases of digital goods: No
- Contains ads: Yes

**Account deletion**

- Web link: https://jcanstudios.github.io/stack-the-cats/delete-data.html
- In-app: Settings → Delete account (opens the web page).

**Government apps, financial features, health, news:** No to all.

## 3. Data safety

**Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** Yes
**Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?** Yes
**Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?** Yes (the web link above)

Data types to tick (shared = sent to a third party for its own use; here only Google AdMob, for ads):

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Optional? | Purposes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Location → Approximate location | Yes | Yes | Required | Advertising or marketing, Analytics, Fraud prevention |
| Personal info → Name | Yes | No | Optional (sign-in) | App functionality, Account management |
| Personal info → Email address | Yes | No | Optional (sign-in) | Account management |
| Personal info → User IDs | Yes | No | Optional (sign-in) | App functionality, Account management |
| Personal info → Other info (profile photo link) | Yes | No | Optional (sign-in) | App functionality |
| App activity → App interactions | Yes | Yes | Required | App functionality, Analytics, Advertising or marketing, Fraud prevention |
| App info and performance → Diagnostics | Yes | Yes | Required | Analytics, Advertising or marketing, Fraud prevention |
| Device or other IDs | Yes | Yes | Required | Advertising or marketing, Analytics, Fraud prevention |

Where these come from:

- Approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics and device IDs: the Google Mobile Ads SDK (its own data disclosure lists IP address, user product interactions, diagnostic information and the advertising ID / app set ID, all collected and shared, encrypted in transit). Firebase App Check also checks the device to stop cheating.
- Name, email, user IDs and photo link: Google or Facebook sign-in, stored in Firebase for the player's account. Scores, game progress and friends are part of "App interactions" (used for app functionality).
- Not collected: precise location, contacts, messages, photos and files, financial info, health, calendar, web history. Facebook is used for sign-in only, with app-event reporting and its advertising ID collection turned off.

**Data is processed ephemerally?** No.
